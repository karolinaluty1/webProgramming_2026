Final Project Proposal: GlowCheck

Skincare Routine Builder & Active Ingredient Compatibility Checker

1. Project Idea & Problem Statement

Many skincare enthusiasts buy popular cosmetic products without knowing how active ingredients interact. Combining strong actives such as Retinol and AHA/BHA exfoliating acids in the same routine can damage the skin barrier.

GlowCheck will be an interactive web application that helps users build safe morning (AM) and evening (PM) skincare routines, check ingredient conflicts, and keep track of open product expiration dates.

2. Core Planned Features

What the website will do:

  • Ingredient Conflict Detector: Select two or more active ingredients (e.g., Vitamin C, Niacinamide, Retinol, Salicylic Acid) to see if they are safe to layer together.
  • AM / PM Routine Sorter: Automatically orders selected cosmetics from thinnest to thickest consistency (cleanser → toner → serum → moisturizer → SPF).
  • Skin Type Filter: Highlights formulas tailored for dry, oily, combination, or sensitive skin.

Target Audience & Scope

Designed for beginners and skincare hobbyists who want a clean, visual tool instead of searching through complicated dermatology blogs.
